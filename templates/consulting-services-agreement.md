# Consulting Services Agreement

This Consulting Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of {{effective_date}} (the "Effective Date") by and between **{{client_name}}**, a {{client_entity_type}} with an address at {{client_address}} (the "Client"), and **{{consultant_name}}**, a {{consultant_entity_type}} with an address at {{consultant_address}} (the "Consultant").

## 1. Engagement and Scope

The Client engages the Consultant to provide the consulting services described in one or more statements of work signed by both parties (each, a "Statement of Work"). The initial Statement of Work is attached as Exhibit A. If this Agreement conflicts with a Statement of Work, this Agreement controls unless the Statement of Work expressly states otherwise.

## 2. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for {{term_months}} month(s), and then renews automatically for successive periods of the same length unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and Payment

(a) **Fees.** The Client shall pay the Consultant at the rate of {{hourly_rate}} per hour, or as otherwise specified in the applicable Statement of Work.
(b) **Invoicing.** The Consultant shall invoice monthly in arrears, and the Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within {{payment_terms_days}} days of receipt.
(c) **Expenses.** The Client shall reimburse reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses supported by receipts.
(d) **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of taxes. Each party is responsible for its own income taxes.

## 4. Relationship of the Parties

The Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship. The Consultant is not authorized to bind the Client.

## 5. Intellectual Property

Deliverables identified in a Statement of Work become the property of the Client upon full payment for them, and the Consultant assigns to the Client all intellectual property rights in those deliverables. The Consultant retains all rights in its pre-existing materials, tools, and general know-how, and grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any of them that are incorporated into the deliverables.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other party's non-public information that it receives under this Agreement with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or receive the services, and not disclose it except to personnel and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement, and for trade secrets, as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 7. Warranties

The Consultant warrants that the services will be performed in a professional manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, (a) NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, AND (b) EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IS LIMITED TO THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE TO THE CONSULTANT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience on {{termination_notice_days}} days' written notice, or immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after notice. The Client shall pay for all services performed and approved expenses incurred through the effective date of termination.
