# Promissory Note

**Principal Amount:** {{principal_amount}}
**Date:** {{effective_date}}

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **{{borrower_name}}**, with an address at {{borrower_address}} (the "Borrower"), promises to pay to the order of **{{lender_name}}**, with an address at {{lender_address}} (the "Lender"), the principal sum of {{principal_amount}}, together with interest as provided below.

## 1. Interest

The unpaid principal balance of this Note accrues simple interest at the rate of {{interest_rate_percent}}% per year, starting on the date above, until paid in full. Interest is calculated on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed. In no event will the interest rate exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

## 2. Repayment

The Borrower shall repay this Note as follows: {{repayment_schedule}}. All unpaid principal and accrued interest are due and payable in full no later than {{maturity_date}} (the "Maturity Date").

## 3. Prepayment

The Borrower may prepay all or any part of this Note at any time without penalty. Prepayments are applied first to accrued interest and then to principal.

## 4. Late Payment

If any payment is more than {{grace_period_days}} days late, the Borrower shall pay a late charge of {{late_fee}}.

## 5. Default

Each of the following is an "Event of Default": (a) the Borrower fails to make any payment when due and does not cure the failure within {{grace_period_days}} days; or (b) the Borrower becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding. Upon an Event of Default, the Lender may, by written notice to the Borrower, declare the entire unpaid principal and accrued interest immediately due and payable.

## 6. Collection Costs

If the Lender takes action to collect amounts due under this Note after an Event of Default, the Borrower shall pay the Lender's reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent permitted by law.

## 7. General

(a) **Waiver.** The Borrower waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, and protest. No delay by the Lender in exercising any right waives that right.
(b) **Governing Law.** This Note is governed by the laws of {{governing_law}}.
(c) **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.
(d) **Assignment.** The Lender may assign this Note. The Borrower may not assign its obligations without the Lender's prior written consent.
