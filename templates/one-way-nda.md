# Non-Disclosure Agreement (One-Way)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of {{effective_date}} (the "Effective Date") by and between **{{discloser_name}}**, with an address at {{discloser_address}} (the "Discloser"), and **{{recipient_name}}**, with an address at {{recipient_address}} (the "Recipient").

## 1. Purpose

The Discloser intends to disclose certain confidential information to the Recipient solely for the purpose of {{purpose}} (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient, in any form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. It includes, without limitation, business, financial, technical, and product information, know-how, and trade secrets.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient;
(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure by the Discloser;
(c) is lawfully obtained by the Recipient from a third party without a confidentiality obligation; or
(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Confidential Information.

## 4. Recipient's Obligations

The Recipient shall:

(a) hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence and use it only for the Purpose;
(b) not disclose it to anyone other than its representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement;
(c) take reasonable measures to protect the Confidential Information from unauthorized use or disclosure; and
(d) promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorized use or disclosure of which it becomes aware.

If the Recipient is legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, where legally permitted, give the Discloser prompt notice and disclose only what is legally required.

## 5. Term

The Recipient's obligations under this Agreement continue for {{confidentiality_period_years}} year(s) from the Effective Date, except that obligations regarding trade secrets continue for as long as the information qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.

## 6. Return of Materials

Upon the Discloser's request, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information and, on request, certify the same in writing.

## 7. No License

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No license or other right is granted to the Recipient except as expressly stated in this Agreement.

## 8. Remedies

The Recipient acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the Discloser irreparable harm, and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 9. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of {{governing_law}}. It is the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter, may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties, and may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
